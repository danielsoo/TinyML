**Changes by item**

1. **Final model comparison.**
   - New Table 3 lists each principal deployable model (P, QC, R, S80, SQC, with the float teacher as reference) with recall, achieved test FAR, size, latency on both chips and training cost, for the random splits and unseen hosts.
   - Supplement S1 and S8 link every reported number to its exported file, SHA-256 and conversion setting.
   - A new paragraph in Section 4 states the conditions under which S80 suffices and where R differs, rather than claiming general equivalence.
2. **Supporting evidence.**
   - The supplement (PDF plus CSV files, generated by `scripts/build_supplement.py` from existing records) contains:
     - S1: per-run results at both budgets, with export identifiers
     - S2: run-paired differences
     - S3: device disagreements classified into additional misses, additional detections, added or avoided false alarms, and unlabelled probes, by model, board, budget and input category
     - S4: memory and latency breakdown
     - S5: stream completeness and resets
     - S6: runtime comparison
     - S7: split provenance and freeze history
     - S8: deployable models
   - Table 4 (unseen hosts) now gives the achieved test FAR of every model, and the paper cites the supplement where each record applies.
3. **Test-set independence.**
   - The introduction no longer says "disjoint from everything used during development". It now states that the test records were excluded from training, threshold selection and calibration of every reported model, and that they had been in the training pool of our earlier exploratory pipeline.
   - Section 3.1 adds the freeze history and marks the post hoc additions: the QC/SQC arms, the E4 clipped-FP32 and 16-bit rows, the unseen-host split, the per-tensor re-exports and the runtime comparison. The full timeline is Supplement S7b.
4. **Runtime conclusions.** Section 7, the abstract, the introduction and the conclusion now state only what was demonstrated: on the classic ESP32 with the 4,462 TON_IoT inputs, the tested per-channel S80 model improved to 4,459 matching decisions, and the per-tensor S80 model kept complete agreement with bit-identical outputs. The paper says explicitly that the other models and the ESP32-S3 were not run on the current runtime.
5. **Numerical evidence and the int16 failure.**
   - Section 5.2 now gives the actual execution path of E3: each cell is an exported integer model (float32 input → QUANTIZE → four FULLY_CONNECTED layers with int8 activations, per-tensor int8 weights and int32 biases → LOGISTIC → DEQUANTIZE), run by the TFLite interpreter. "QAT float" is identified as the only fake-quantized simulation.
   - For the int16 model, Section 7 reports only the observed constant output equal to the sigmoid of the output-layer bias.
   - Table 8 no longer presents its latency or arena as a deployment cost; its recovery is labelled host-only.
6. **Claims aligned with the evidence.**
   - Validation budgets and achieved test FAR are now distinguished throughout.
   - The unseen-host recall loss (1.6–4.4 points) and the unseen-host FAR behaviour are stated: one 121-flow benign cluster makes the teacher's FAR 4.77% in two runs, while every INT8 model with a bounded input range has 0%.
   - The accuracy trade-off of clipping is stated in Section 6, Table 8 and Table 11.
   - R vs. P is attributed to the complete recipe, noting that QC (clipped QAT without distillation) is within 0.65 points of R.
   - The one-client result is limited to our label-balanced four-client partition.

While reviewing the records, we also corrected a few statements. None changes a principal conclusion.
- **E7 exceptions.** We previously wrote that per-tensor models had "one exception in 2,700" inputs outside the near-threshold set. The records show at most two per model and budget (CIC QC80 at 1%, CIC P80 at 5%). The text now says so and states that only one additional missed attack occurred in total.
- **Daggers in Table 2.** The † marks on the 128 KB P and the one-client P entries were unnecessary: those exports are per-tensor. The † now marks only S128.
- **Exports behind the McNemar tests and the E2 transitions.** These were computed on the first-selected exports; for some P/S80/F8 runs the reported per-tensor exports differ. S2 marks every such test, and the E2 captions already say so. The paper cites only the run-paired differences of the reported exports.
